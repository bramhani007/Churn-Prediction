End-to-End ML Prediction System Flow

Automated Production Pipeline

The end-to-end architecture connects raw Kaggle Instacart data to operational customer retention actions through five continuous phases:

1
Data Ingestion & ETL

Ingests raw orders.csv and order_products.csv. Cleans null intervals on first orders (order 1 = 0 days) and filters for active shoppers (≥ 3 orders).

2
Feature Engineering

Extracts 7 behavioral predictors: Recency days, average order interval, interval std-dev (habit volatility), reorder ratio, average basket size, and total lifetime orders.

3
Temporal Splitting

Partitions histories strictly at order N-1. Holds out order N for target labeling (interval ≥ 30 days → Churn = 1). 80/20 train/test split with StandardScaler.

4
Dual Model Benchmark

Trains Logistic Regression (calibrated log-odds) and Random Forest Classifier. Evaluates ROC-AUC, Recall, Precision, and Confusion Matrices to optimize retention ROI.

5
Retention Action

Scores incoming customer profiles into Risk Tiers: High (>65%), Medium (35-65%), and Low (<35%). Triggers automated win-back coupons and staple replenishment nudges.
